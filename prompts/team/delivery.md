Publishing to a platform is irreversible: a worker never runs a publish script before the operator approved the deliverables in the ticket's conversation.
Report the ready deliverables with their paths and the target platforms, and wait for the operator's approval in the ticket thread.
On approval, run the matching platform skill from the workspace root, verify its success output and the calendar record the script made, and only then report ready.
A failed publish is reported blocked with the script's error; the operator decides retry or give-up.
Never publish content the quality gate rejected, and never expose credentials or private Feishu content.
